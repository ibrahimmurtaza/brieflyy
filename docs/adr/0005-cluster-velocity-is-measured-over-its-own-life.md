# Cluster velocity is measured over the Cluster's own life, not the window

A Cluster's velocity is its Stories per day, divided by the time since the first of those Stories was seen rather than by the Topic's whole window. The glossary says Active is a matter of velocity being "above a threshold" without saying what the number is, and the denominator is the part that decides whether the distinction means anything: over a fixed seven-day window a single Story always scores 1/7 and a busy week scores 1, so the number says more about the length of the window than about whether a story is moving. Over the Cluster's own life, a story that broke this morning and a story that was covered once and never again both have one Story, and only the first is news.

That makes the threshold a real line rather than a formality. It sits at `CLUSTER_ACTIVE_VELOCITY_THRESHOLD` (0.2 Stories per day): two Stories inside the default window clear it, one Story that has not been re-seen since the window opened does not. A cluster is re-formed from scratch on every ingest cycle and anything the new formation does not include is archived, so a Cluster's velocity decays as it ages without new Stories and it drops out of the LivingBrief on its own. The span is floored at one day (`MIN_VELOCITY_SPAN_DAYS`), so a Cluster first seen minutes ago is measured as a whole day rather than divided by a fraction of one and scoring above anything it has earned.
