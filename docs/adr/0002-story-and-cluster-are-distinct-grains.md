# Story and Cluster are distinct grains

A Story is a deduped event — a group of near-duplicate Articles within a 48–72h window, identified by a story-fingerprint (named entities + key phrases). A Cluster is a grouping of related Stories within a 7d window, and is the unit the User actually sees. The two are recorded as separate concepts in `CONTEXT.md` because the pipeline deals in Stories and the product deals in Clusters, and conflating them would force the ranking, feedback, and summarization layers to agree on a grain that neither layer actually wants. Story-level dedup collapses syndication noise; Cluster-level grouping gives the User a coherent narrative rather than a stream of deduped fragments.

**How Clusters are grouped was decided later, and changed twice.** This ADR only fixes the grain and is still current; the mechanism is [[0006-cluster-grouping-is-transitive-closure]] (transitive closure, itself superseded) and then [[0030-a-cluster-is-grouped-by-weighted-similarity]] (IDF-weighted similarity with a constrained linkage), which is what runs now.
